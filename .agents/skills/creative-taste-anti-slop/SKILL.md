# Creative Taste: Eradicate AI Component Slop

Guidance for crafting creative, editorial, and high-impact web pages that look human-crafted and avoid formulaic AI styling.

## The Anti-Slop Blacklist

### 1. Banned Visual Patterns
- **No Neon Purple/Cyan Gradients**: Eradicate generic AI gradient text (`bg-gradient-to-r from-purple-500 to-indigo-600`) and glowing backdrop blur orbs behind hero sections.
- **No Pill Button Overuse**: Stop slapping `rounded-full` onto large hero buttons unless explicitly part of an established design identity.
- **No Generic Bento Grids**: Avoid forcing features into meaningless 3x2 bento boxes filled with meaningless SVG icons.
- **No Faux Glassmorphism**: Don't use frosted-glass cards with thick white borders on dark backgrounds unless justified by physical depth.

### 2. High-Taste Fundamentals
- **Restrained Color Palettes**: Master monochromatic depth, warm slates, or high-contrast duotones before introducing accent colors.
- **Editorial Typography**: Hero sections should rely on intentional typographic scale, negative space, and crisp tracking (`letter-spacing: -0.02em` on display headings).
- **Asymmetry & Intentional Breaking**: Break rigid grid boxes with intentional asymmetric alignments, offset dividers, and stark typographic hierarchies.
- **Tactile Borders**: Thin, crisp, 1px borders with subtle alpha blending (`rgba(255, 255, 255, 0.08)` or solid muted tones) rather than heavy drop shadows.
