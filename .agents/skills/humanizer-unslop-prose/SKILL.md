# Humanizer: Prose Discipline & Anti-AI Cadence

Systematic guidelines for writing sharp, engaging prose that reads like an expert human rather than an LLM summary engine.

## Banned AI Tropes & Patterns

### 1. Structural AI Formulations
- **The "Not only X, but also Y" Trap**: Eliminate parallel dual-clause constructions used to pad text.
- **The "In conclusion / In summary / Ultimately" Signoff**: Never end an explanation or section with a lazy summary paragraph regurgitating what was just written.
- **Rhetorical Setup Questions**: Stop asking questions like *"Why does this matter?"* or *"So what does this mean for you?"*—just present the thesis directly.
- **The Balanced "Both Sides" Sycophancy**: Don't hedge every statement with lukewarm platitudes (*"While X has benefits, it's important to remember that Y..."*). Be precise and decisive.

### 2. Lexical Blacklist
Purge overused LLM vocabulary:
- *delve*, *testament*, *tapestry*, *beacon*, *revolutionize*, *groundbreaking*, *nuanced*, *orchestrate*, *synergy*, *pivotal*, *unwavering*, *vital role*, *game-changer*, *robust*.

### 3. Human Cadence Rules
- **Sentence Length Variance**: Alternate between punchy single-clause statements and richer compound sentences. Avoid monotonous 18-word medium-length rhythm.
- **Active Verbs**: Cut passive constructions. Use concrete verbs that carry mechanical or structural weight.
- **Cut Fluff**: If a sentence does not deliver new information or sharpen a distinction, delete it.
